WIND IN THE ARENA THAT MEANS WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE

Two interventions in this project have done something. Wind steadies the
turning -- a quarter less circling and a fifth straighter walking, the only
addition that improves behaviour rather than costing it. The forage arena
removed the tall dark bar the model kept walking into, which turned "it
approaches dark verticals" into the sharper "it approaches large structure of
either polarity".

They had never been combined, and there was a reason to expect more than the
sum: wind's benefit is course stabilisation, it steadies HOW the animal turns,
and in the fly arena that steadiness was spent walking more reliably into a
wall. Remove the wall's pull and a steadier course has somewhere better to go.

Isolated properly: identical to behav_forage -- same arena, same 60 seeds, same
vision-gated odour, same controls -- with wind and nothing else added.

THE GROUP MEANS LOOKED LIKE A RESULT.

    vs matched random arm        intact            mirrored
      forage, no wind         -5.00 +- 7.13      +5.13 +- 7.36
      forage + wind           +3.93 +- 8.14      +0.93 +- 7.60

    intact minus mirrored, same seeds
      forage, no wind         -3.47 +- 7.36    (inverted)
      forage + wind           +3.40 +- 9.57    (normal order)

Wind appears to lift the intact arm by nine points, push the mirrored arm down
by four, and flip the inverted dissociation back to the direction it has
everywhere else in this project. Four measures all moving the same way.

THE PER-SEED DISTRIBUTION REFUTES IT. The right test for "wind restores the
dissociation" is a difference in differences: per seed, the wind effect on the
intact model minus the wind effect on the mirrored one.

    health          +6.87 +- 11.03    21 of 60 seeds positive, sign test p=0.993
    tics survived  +27.70 +- 36.97    26 of 60 seeds positive, p=0.877
    damage taken    +4.83 +- 10.04    22 of 60 seeds positive, p=0.986

The mean is positive and the MEDIAN IS NEGATIVE. Only a third of seeds show the
effect at all; the group mean is carried by a handful of large ones. A sign
test at p=0.993 is not a near miss, it is the opposite direction dominating.

NULL. Wind and the forage arena do not combine. The movement measures agree --
paired against behav_forage, chatter +0.002 +- 0.012, spin -0.016 +- 0.025,
collisions -0.64 +- 2.11, none significant -- so this is not a case of the
health column missing something the movement column would show, which is the
failure mode this project has already had once.

WHY, AND THE PREMISE WAS ALREADY REFUTED BEFORE THE RUN.

It is not a floor effect. Starting spin is the same in both arenas, 0.167 in
the fly arena and 0.164 here, so there was equal room to improve. What differs
is how strongly wind acts:

    arena     spin no wind   wind effect on spin   vision->steer r
    fly           0.167      -0.0391 +- 0.0296*        +0.037
    forage        0.164      -0.0161 +- 0.0254         -0.084

The wind effect is two and a half times weaker here and loses significance, and
the column that explains it is the last one: the visual steering correlation
has OPPOSITE SIGN in the two arenas, because the forage arena inverted the
polarity of the structure the model steers by.

That matters because wind's stabilisation was already known not to be
standalone. The arm controls in behav_wind show it only appears with the retina
intact -- mirrored +0.009 +- 0.035, frozen -0.034 +- 0.037, neither
significant. Airflow does not substitute for a visual course signal; it works
WITH one. Invert that signal and the two stop pulling together.

So the prediction this run was built on -- that wind's steadiness was being
wasted walking into walls, and would pay off once the walls stopped pulling --
assumed the benefit was independent of vision and would carry across arenas.
The arm controls had already shown it is not, two days before this ran. The
experiment was worth doing and the premise was inconsistent with a result
already in this directory.

The mechanism above is consistent with the data rather than proven by it. The
direct test is wind in an arena where the visual steering sign is positive
again, which is buildable, since the sign follows the walls' contrast polarity.

WORTH KEEPING FOR THE METHOD. A mean of +6.87 with 21 of 60 seeds positive is
exactly what an outlier-driven non-result looks like, and quoting the mean
alone would have made it a finding. Report the per-seed sign alongside any
paired mean in this project; the seeds are levels, and levels differ enough
that a few can carry an average on their own.
