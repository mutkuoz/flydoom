THE OPTOMOTOR REFLEX AFTER THE TWO INTERFACE FIXES

What changed (both opt-in, neither touches the brain):
  anatomical eye map  120-degree hex axes, eyes mirrored, placed on the head
                      as measured (flydoom/retina.py, experiments/m16_eye_axes.py)
  fixed turn sign     steer TOWARD the more active side; ViZDoom's turn delta
                      is positive for a right turn, not a left one
Both matter for this reflex specifically: with the eyes unmirrored a rotating
world drives both eyes the same way, which is the one stimulus the optomotor
response has to tell apart, and the reversed sign then applies whatever is left
backwards.

CLOSED LOOP (run.sh, 6 seeds, 350 tics, drum reversing every 70 tics)
Modulation is commanded yaw during the right-turning half minus the left half,
each seed against its own no-drum baseline. Optomotor = negative.

  condition                          1 deg/tic      2            4
  B  both fixes                      +0.18+-0.28   +0.06+-0.37  -0.01+-0.18
  B  both fixes, eyes mirrored       +1.26+-0.29*  +1.02+-0.32* +0.69+-0.32*
  B  both fixes, eyes frozen         +0.12+-0.11*  +0.00+-0.05  +0.06+-0.09
  C  eye fix only (legacy turn sign) +0.68+-0.45*  +0.26+-0.13* -0.12+-0.18
  D  turn fix only (lattice eye map)  -0.41+-1.01  +0.12+-0.32  +0.26+-0.56

Flipping the chain in exactly one place (C, or B mirrored) gives a large
speed-tuned response in the WRONG direction; flipping it in two places (B) or
zero (D) gives nothing. That is what a directional pathway plus a sign error
looks like -- but closed loop cannot establish the sign, because a correct
counter-turn cancels its own stimulus while a wrong-way turn amplifies it.
Hence the tethered test.

TETHERED / OPEN LOOP (run_open.sh, 10 seeds, 350 tics) -- THE REAL TEST
Commands are recorded and discarded, the body turns only as the drum dictates,
and there is no walking, so the retinal image is the drum's alone. Both the
command and the raw DNp15 left-right difference are recorded, the latter
because the decoder's deadzone and 3 s baseline filter can remove a real
signal before it reaches the command.

  command modulation, minus each seed's own no-drum value
    both fixes            1: +0.06+-0.18   2: +0.09+-0.18   4: +0.39+-0.19*  8: -0.05+-0.18
    eyes mirrored         1: -0.04+-0.18   2: +0.09+-0.20   4: -0.01+-0.19   8: +0.08+-0.27
    eyes frozen           1:  0.00+-0.00   2:  0.00+-0.00   4:  0.00+-0.00   8:  0.00+-0.00
    lattice eye map       1: +0.11+-0.18   2: -0.01+-0.13   4: -0.02+-0.16   8: -0.04+-0.20

  DNp15 L-R modulation (Hz), same subtraction
    both fixes            1: +0.39+-0.50   2: +0.20+-0.52   4: -0.14+-0.46   8: +0.20+-0.57
    eyes mirrored         1: +0.34+-0.47   2: +0.02+-0.59   4: +0.33+-0.58   8: +0.20+-0.74

NEGATIVE. Nothing separates except one cell of sixteen (+0.39 at 4 deg/tic),
which points the wrong way and does not reverse when the eyes are mirrored.
The frozen arm returning exactly zero is the sanity check that the bookkeeping
is sound: a frozen retina makes both half-cycles identical.

WHY, from the grating test (m3_optomotor.py --eye-map ..., open loop, no Doom)
Direction SEPARATION at the horizontal system, rightward minus leftward drift:

                                     lattice map   anatomical map
  all-applied (spiking T4, gain 16)     1.625 Hz       3.375 Hz
  frozen model (graded T4, gain 1)      0.500 Hz       0.375 Hz

The corrected eye map doubles the separation in the configuration behaviour
uses, which is the fix doing exactly what it should. It is still 3 Hz against a
STANDING left-right asymmetry of about 20 Hz, and it does not reverse sign with
drift direction. Per-cell selectivity is unchanged (|DSI| ~0.007), as expected:
the eye map fixes where each lens looks, not what a detector computes.

READING
The two bugs were real, and this is the honest size of their effect: the fly's
picture of the world is now correct, the population direction signal doubles,
and the reflex still does not appear. The limit is the one the paper already
documents -- per-cell direction selectivity capped near 0.08 where a fly needs
~0.5 -- and no interface fix reaches it.
