Each modality has its own descending pathway, and they are not the same one.

Odour on against odour off, all-applied visual configuration, single seed,
130 scored tics:

  readout        odour off   odour on     change in L-R
  DNa02 L-R        -27.89      -5.70          +22.19
  DNp15 L-R        -40.54     -39.79           +0.75
  DNp01 L-R        -72.70     -72.99           -0.29

Smell moves the steering asymmetry of DNa02 by 22 Hz and leaves DNp15
untouched. Vision is the other way round: the T4/T5-dependent directional
signal reaches DNp15 at 32 Hz while DNa02 draws 2.3% of its input from visual
populations and 0.34% from the horizontal system.

This matches the published characterisations rather than cutting against them.
DNa02 steers goal-directed walking under central-complex control, and
odour-guided navigation IS goal-directed steering. DNp15 (DNHS1) is the
optomotor neuron the horizontal system drives. The model has been reading one
descending pathway while delivering the other modality's stimulus.

Consequence for klinotaxis: it must be measured with the DNa02 readout, not
DNp15. Smell carries how close and never which way, since the antennae are
0.3 mm apart and receive identical drive, so an animal finds a source by
moving and comparing concentration against its own recent past, turning when
it falls. The existing run gives connectome r = +0.058 +- 0.117 against
shuffled r = -0.095 +- 0.074 on six episodes, which is the wrong sign and
under-powered, and it predates the corrected visual configuration.

  DNa02   +20.62 +- 1.83   *   per seed: 22.2 20.1 20.1 21.4 16.7 23.2
  DNp15    -0.12 +- 1.12       per seed:  0.8 -1.6 -0.4  2.2 -0.5 -1.1
  DNp01    +0.55 +- 1.12       per seed: -0.3 -1.1  0.1  2.9  0.5  1.2

Every seed moves DNa02 in the same direction, and the effect is an order of
magnitude larger than its own between-seed spread. Neither of the other two
readouts moves at all. This is the largest and most consistent sensorimotor
coupling measured anywhere in this project, larger than any produced by
vision, and it sits on the descending neuron the literature assigns to
goal-directed steering, which is the pathway odour navigation should use.

Still to establish: whether it produces behaviour. A 21 Hz modulation of the
steering command is a coupling, not a turn toward an odour source, and the
klinotaxis measurement is the test.
