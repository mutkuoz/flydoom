THE BEHAVIOUR TABLES WITH A FORWARD-WALKING FLY

Why this was re-run. Every behavioural result before this was measured through
an interface with three errors: the eye map read with the wrong hexagonal
convention and unmirrored, a reversed turn sign, and MDN's tonic 107 Hz (driven
by the optic gain of 16) read as a walk command, so the agent reversed on 97%
of tics. Its matched random agent reversed too, so the comparisons were fair,
but the behaviour was not a fly's.

What changed: the interface only (anatomical eye map, correct turn sign, MDN
read as bursts, antennal contact on forward pushes). Same brain configuration,
same 60 held-out seeds (40-99), same controls. Full table: RESULT.txt
(scripts/analyse_fixed.py). run.sh rebuilds it.

READING

1. In the sky arena the old result does not survive. With a correct interface
   the intact fly does no better than chance on any measure. Its walls look
   flat grey at the fly's resolution, so walking face-first into one leaves
   nothing to steer by; and head to head on the same levels the intact fly
   covers 3.5 fewer tiles and is stuck 9 points more than the mirrored one.
   The old configuration's advantage came partly from its errors: reversing
   with a reversed turn sign steers the direction of travel toward the more
   active side, and it faced away from the wall it was pressed against.

2. In the fly arena -- walls striped at the period the motion detectors
   prefer, bright textured ground -- the health and survival advantage comes
   back (+7.53 health, +34.0 tics, both significant, with more damage taken,
   the same profile as the original headline), and mirroring removes it
   (-0.67, -13.1). Freezing weakens it to non-significance (+4.47, +22.6),
   so the frozen control is ambiguous rather than clean.

3. The same limit as before: no model-versus-model difference in health or
   survival is individually significant (fly arena, intact minus mirrored,
   health +3.00 +- 6.18). The dissociation is in the advantage over the
   matched control.

4. Exploration and stuck-time advantages belong to the mirrored and frozen
   flies, not the intact one, in both arenas. The intact fly is stuck more
   than the mirrored one in both (sky +0.09*, fly +0.08*). With correct
   geometry its vision steers it toward large dark structure, which in Doom
   means walls. Real flies are drawn to large dark vertical objects; here that
   is maladaptive, and the extra damage it takes (+8.63*) is consistent with it
   approaching objects indiscriminately, poison included. A hypothesis, not a
   measured mechanism.

Net: a vision-dependent advantage over chance exists with a correct interface
and a forward-walking fly, but only in an arena whose structure the fly can
resolve, only on health and survival, and with an ambiguous frozen control.
It is weaker and narrower than the claim it replaces.
