DOES IT HOLD A DARK BAR IN FRONT OF IT?

Two visual behaviours have been tested in this project and both came back
empty, and they share a cause: direction selectivity and looming are both
computations on the ORDER in which neighbouring ommatidia fire, and order in
time is what this model loses. Fixation is not. Working out which side an
object is on needs retinotopy, a spatial fact, verified here end to end. M7
already found the neural half: LC10a's left-right difference tracks target
azimuth. This is the behavioural half, and it is the experiment a fly
laboratory would run first.

THE ARENA (scripts/build_stripe_arena.py). A 72-sided cylinder, radius 512,
uniformly bright, with one dark bar 20 degrees wide, and the fly at the centre.
At constant distance Doom's light diminishing is constant, so the bar is the
only azimuthal feature in the world. Tethered: the brain's forward and lateral
commands are recorded and discarded and only its yaw reaches the body, so the
bar's retinal position is a pure integral of what the brain commanded.
stripe_blank is the same cylinder with no bar.

Measured: F = mean cos(bar azimuth). +1 is the bar always ahead, -1 always
behind, 0 no preference. 20 held-out seeds (40-59), 1500 tics, 300 dropped.

  arm        n   vector length   |held azimuth|          F
  intact    20       0.965            83.2        +0.128 +- 0.278
  mirrored  20       0.961            94.7        -0.051 +- 0.333
  frozen    20       0.980            68.1        +0.299 +- 0.309

NULL, and the frozen arm is why.

1. The fly DOES lock the bar at a stable azimuth. Vector length 0.965 means
   that once settled it holds the bar within a few degrees, for a thousand
   tics, while turning 1.3 deg/tic and moving on 78% of them. That looked like
   a result.

2. It is not vision. A fly whose retina is frozen on one frame locks just as
   tightly (0.980) and its F is no lower. The explanation is in the decoder,
   not the eye: the yaw channel subtracts a 3 s baseline, so any sustained
   command is removed, and the only fixed point of the loop is "stop turning".
   The heading then freezes wherever it happens to be, and since the bar is
   fixed in the world, a frozen heading IS a locked bar azimuth. The measure
   cannot tell the two apart.

3. Nor is the azimuth it holds a fixation azimuth. Intact holds the bar 83
   degrees off centre on average, not ahead. There is one suggestive thing --
   6 of 20 intact seeds land between +65 and +73 degrees, and the frozen arm
   has no such cluster (70% of intact seeds within 45 degrees of +-70, against
   45% for frozen and mirrored) -- but that is one cluster in one arm of three
   at n=20, and this note is not going to make a claim out of it.

WHAT THE EXPERIMENT SHOULD HAVE BEEN, and now also is. An equilibrium measure
has one number per episode and a control that reproduces it. The open-loop
version has 1200: turn the body at a constant rate so the bar walks all the way
round the eye, run it both ways so that any response to the rotation itself
cancels, and read the commanded turn as a function of where the bar is. A fly
that steers toward the bar gives a curve that falls through zero. That is the
measurement with the power, and it is what --sweep does.
