THE ONE BEHAVIOURAL COMPARISON THE ODOUR CONFOUND DOES NOT TOUCH

Connectome against its own command-matched AR(1) control, 30 held-out seeds,
all-applied configuration. Both arms run with odour on, so the trajectory
divergence that invalidates the odour on/off comparison does not apply here:
the manipulation is the command source, not the stimulus.

  healed           +12.67 +- 10.80  *
  tics             +37.90 +- 36.60  *
  tiles_visited     +3.93 +-  3.05  *
  stuck_frac        -0.07 +-  0.06  *
  damage           +10.87 +-  8.51  *   (worse)

The model collects more health, survives longer, explores more and gets stuck
less than a control with matched command statistics, while taking more damage.
That is a genuine behavioural advantage and it is the strongest such result in
the project.

What it does NOT establish is that the advantage is visual. The mirrored-retina
arm for this configuration was killed mid-run when the speed-matched test was
chained in front of it, and never re-run. Mirroring reverses every horizontal
optic-flow signal while leaving firing rates, contrast statistics and the
wiring untouched, so an advantage that survives it is not coming from optic
flow. Queued now.

FULL MIRROR CONTROL, 30 PAIRED SEEDS

Connectome minus its own command-matched AR(1) control, in each condition:

  metric              normal retina        mirrored retina
  healed              +12.67 +- 10.80 *    -0.53 +- 7.37
  tics                +37.90 +- 36.60 *    -5.47 +- 31.88
  tiles_visited        +3.93 +-  3.05 *    -0.57 +- 3.71
  stuck_frac           -0.07 +-  0.06 *    +0.01 +- 0.08
  damage              +10.87 +-  8.51 *    -0.87 +- 7.73

Every advantage present under a normal retina is absent under a mirrored one.
Mirroring flips the retinal sampling grid horizontally, reversing every
horizontal optic-flow signal while leaving firing rates, contrast statistics
and the wiring untouched, so an advantage arising from motor command
statistics would survive it. None does.

THE QUALIFICATION THAT MUST TRAVEL WITH THIS. The direct connectome-against-
connectome comparison between conditions is directionally consistent but NOT
significant: healed -8.00 +- 10.84, tics -29.70 +- 37.59, tiles -1.83 +- 3.61.
The dissociation is therefore in the difference-of-differences, the advantage
over control, rather than in the model's absolute performance, and the
command-matched control itself changes under mirroring because it is matched
to the mirrored model's own command statistics.

The defensible claim is: under an intact retina the model outperforms a
command-matched control on health collected, survival, exploration and
stuck-fraction, and under a mirrored retina it outperforms it on none. Whether
its absolute performance falls is not established at this sample size.

One oddity recorded rather than explained: collisions per 1k tics is the one
metric that becomes favourable under mirroring, -3.62 +- 3.01, having been
+0.05 +- 2.87 normally.

ABSOLUTE VALUES: THE DISSOCIATION IS TWO-SIDED

                        normal          mirrored
  healed  connectome    24.80 +- 10.26  16.80 +- 6.34
          random        12.13 +-  5.28  17.33 +- 7.46
  tics    connectome   460.27 +- 33.89 430.57 +- 25.56
          random       422.37 +- 22.80 436.03 +- 33.39
  tiles   connectome    21.63 +-  2.99  19.80 +- 2.40
          random        17.70 +-  2.59  20.37 +- 2.96

Under mirroring the model gets worse on all three metrics AND the
command-matched control gets better on all three. The advantage therefore
closes from both ends, and the model's own decline is not individually
significant at this sample (healed -8.00 +- 10.84).

Why the control improves is itself informative rather than a nuisance: it is
matched per seed to the model's own command statistics, so it improving means
that under a mirrored retina the model's commands become ones a random agent
does BETTER with. The command structure stops being advantageous.

So the defensible claim is about command structure rather than raw
performance: with an intact retina the model issues commands that beat a
statistically matched random agent, and with a mirrored retina it does not.
That is vision-dependent, since mirroring changes only the retinal sampling
order, but it is a weaker statement than "performance depends on vision" and
should not be written as the stronger one.

BLIND CONTROL, 30 SEEDS -- CONVERGES WITH THE MIRROR

Connectome minus its own command-matched control:

  metric          intact              mirrored          blind
  healed          +12.67 +- 10.80 *   -0.53 +- 7.37     -5.60 +- 11.57
  tics            +37.90 +- 36.60 *   -5.47 +- 31.88   -33.30 +- 48.33
  tiles_visited    +3.93 +-  3.05 *   -0.57 +- 3.71     +0.47 +-  4.22
  stuck_frac       -0.07 +-  0.06 *   +0.01 +- 0.08     -0.06 +-  0.08

Freezing the retina on one frame abolishes every advantage that mirroring
abolishes. The two controls break vision in different ways -- mirroring
reverses horizontal optic flow while preserving its statistics, freezing
removes optic flow while preserving the mean image -- and they agree.

Absolute health collected by the model is graded across the three conditions,
intact 24.80 > mirrored 16.80 > blind 14.27, which is the ordering expected if
performance scales with how much usable visual signal remains.

The qualification is unchanged and applies to both controls: the model's own
paired decline is directionally consistent and not individually significant
(blind minus intact: healed -10.53 +- 13.31, tics -45.47 +- 52.53).

HEADLINE, STATED AT THE STRENGTH THE DATA SUPPORT

With an intact retina the model issues motor commands that beat a
statistically matched random agent on health collected, survival, exploration
and stuck-fraction. Reversing optic flow abolishes that advantage, and
removing optic flow abolishes it too. The advantage is vision-dependent.

Not claimed: that the model's absolute performance falls significantly without
vision, or that any recognisable fly reflex is present. The optomotor drum
shows no directional response, looming shows none, fixation cells are silent,
and klinotaxis is absent.
