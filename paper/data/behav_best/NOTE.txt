THE ONE BEHAVIOURAL COMPARISON THE ODOUR CONFOUND DOES NOT TOUCH

Connectome against its own command-matched AR(1) control, 30 held-out seeds,
all-applied configuration. Both arms run with odour on, so the trajectory
divergence that invalidates the odour on/off comparison does not apply here:
the manipulation is the command source, not the stimulus.

  healed           +12.67 +- 10.80  *
  tics             +37.90 +- 36.60  *
  tiles_visited     +3.93 +-  3.05  *
  stuck_frac        -0.07 +-  0.06  *
  damage           +10.87 +-  8.51  *   (worse)

The model collects more health, survives longer, explores more and gets stuck
less than a control with matched command statistics, while taking more damage.
That is a genuine behavioural advantage and it is the strongest such result in
the project.

What it does NOT establish is that the advantage is visual. The mirrored-retina
arm for this configuration was killed mid-run when the speed-matched test was
chained in front of it, and never re-run. Mirroring reverses every horizontal
optic-flow signal while leaving firing rates, contrast statistics and the
wiring untouched, so an advantage that survives it is not coming from optic
flow. Queued now.

FULL MIRROR CONTROL, 30 PAIRED SEEDS

Connectome minus its own command-matched AR(1) control, in each condition:

  metric              normal retina        mirrored retina
  healed              +12.67 +- 10.80 *    -0.53 +- 7.37
  tics                +37.90 +- 36.60 *    -5.47 +- 31.88
  tiles_visited        +3.93 +-  3.05 *    -0.57 +- 3.71
  stuck_frac           -0.07 +-  0.06 *    +0.01 +- 0.08
  damage              +10.87 +-  8.51 *    -0.87 +- 7.73

Every advantage present under a normal retina is absent under a mirrored one.
Mirroring flips the retinal sampling grid horizontally, reversing every
horizontal optic-flow signal while leaving firing rates, contrast statistics
and the wiring untouched, so an advantage arising from motor command
statistics would survive it. None does.

THE QUALIFICATION THAT MUST TRAVEL WITH THIS. The direct connectome-against-
connectome comparison between conditions is directionally consistent but NOT
significant: healed -8.00 +- 10.84, tics -29.70 +- 37.59, tiles -1.83 +- 3.61.
The dissociation is therefore in the difference-of-differences, the advantage
over control, rather than in the model's absolute performance, and the
command-matched control itself changes under mirroring because it is matched
to the mirrored model's own command statistics.

The defensible claim is: under an intact retina the model outperforms a
command-matched control on health collected, survival, exploration and
stuck-fraction, and under a mirrored retina it outperforms it on none. Whether
its absolute performance falls is not established at this sample size.

One oddity recorded rather than explained: collisions per 1k tics is the one
metric that becomes favourable under mirroring, -3.62 +- 3.01, having been
+0.05 +- 2.87 normally.
