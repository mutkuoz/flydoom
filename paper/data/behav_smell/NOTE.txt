ODOUR ALONE: NOTHING, ANYWHERE

behav_all applied dendritic cables, a neck and whole-level odour together, and
the vision control collapsed -- a mirrored retina stopped abolishing the
advantage and a frozen retina beat chance outright. Odour was the obvious
suspect, and I wrote it into the paper as the cause before running this. It is
not the cause.

Whole-level odour alone, same 60 seeds, same three arms, nothing else changed:

READING

1. Odour alone produces no advantage in any arm. The frozen arm is the one
   that matters: a BLIND model with odour reaching every medkit in the level at
   five times the old strength scores -0.07. Strong smell does not find food.
   Whatever lifted behav_all's frozen arm to +6.53, it was not this.

2. It makes the intact model WORSE, +3.80 against the plain +7.53. Strengthening
   the odour channel cost it.

   The reason is in the design of that channel and is worth stating plainly.
   flydoom/olfaction.py is bilaterally symmetric on purpose -- the concentration
   is a scalar and every azimuth is discarded, because a direction-carrying
   odour input would have been the cheat the whole experiment exists to avoid.
   A scalar cannot steer. Making it stronger therefore cannot help the model
   FIND anything; it can only add drive that competes with the channel that can.
   That is what the numbers show, and it is a property of the channel rather
   than a surprise about flies.

3. By elimination, the cables or the neck broke the control, and the neck has a
   mechanism the others do not: with a movable head, freezing the FRAME does not
   freeze the INPUT. The eyes keep turning across the held picture, so the
   lenses sample a moving window of a static scene and the arm that is supposed
   to have no vision receives self-generated motion. That would lift the frozen
   arm specifically, which is the arm that moved. paper/data/behav_neck tests it
   directly.
