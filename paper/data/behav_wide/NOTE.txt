THE FULL EYE IN A DAYLIGHT ARENA

What changed (all environment, nothing in the brain):
  - arena: health_gathering_sky, the usual map with open daylight sky in
    place of the dark ceiling (scripts/build_sky_arena.py)
  - full eye: three 170 degree cameras (front, 90 left, 90 right); every one
    of 4,541 lenses reads the world, against 64% at the old 130 degree view
  - corrected lens placement (gnomonic) and per-lens blur (pyramid_blur)
Brain and decoder: the all-applied configuration of behav_best (spiking
T4/T5, optic gain 16, DNp15 yaw, antennal touch, smell on).

Protocol: 60 held-out seeds in two blocks, 40-69 then 70-99, each block run
per condition (run_block1.sh, run_block2.sh). Nothing was selected on either
block; the second block was added after the first came back underpowered,
and is reported combined whatever it showed. The first block alone is in
RESULT_block1.txt. GPU; the behav_best headline ran on CPU in the old arena,
so it is shown for reference and never paired with these runs.

Connectome minus its own command-matched random arm, paired by seed, 95% CI
(* = excludes zero). Full table: RESULT.txt (scripts/analyse_wide.py).

READING
1. With the full eye the model beats its matched control on health and
   survival again, in a second arena, over 60 seeds. Mirroring the eye or
   freezing it removes both. That is the headline's vision-dependent
   advantage, replicated.
2. With the old 130 degree eye in the same arena there is no such advantage.
   In this arena, the effect needs the wider field.
3. Exploration (tiles) and getting stuck less do NOT depend on vision here:
   both survive the frozen eye. They are not evidence of seeing.
4. Same limit as the headline: the dissociation is in the advantage over the
   control. The model's own numbers between conditions are directionally
   consistent (health 21.2 intact, 19.3 mirrored, 19.3 frozen, 16.3 narrow)
   but no connectome-vs-connectome difference in health or survival is
   significant. Full eye minus narrow eye: health +4.93 +- 6.23, survival
   +19.08 +- 27.21.
5. This is performance, not a named fly reflex. The optomotor drum, looming,
   fixation and klinotaxis have not been rerun with the full eye.
