PLAIN + WIND, AND THE ANSWER TO "WHICH ONE CONFIGURATION"

The question this run was built to settle: a real fly is one animal, not a menu
of flags, so which single configuration is the best model of it?

Wind was the candidate addition. It is the only component measured in this
project that improved behaviour rather than costing it -- a quarter less
circling and a fifth straighter walking -- and it had never been run on the
plain configuration, only on top of whole-level odour, which is itself a
handicap. The prediction was recorded in run.sh before the data: keep plain's
advantage (sign test near p=0.005) and gain the stabilisation (spin near
-0.04).

BOTH HALVES FAILED.

  connectome minus its matched random arm, health, sign test over seeds that
  moved (ties excluded -- health is quantised and a third of seeds tie exactly)

    plain        intact     28 up, 10 down, 22 tied     p = 0.005
    plain        mirrored   21 up, 21 down, 18 tied     p = 1.000
    plain+wind   intact     16 up, 18 down, 26 tied     p = 0.864
    plain+wind   mirrored   13 up, 21 down, 26 tied     p = 0.230

  movement, paired against plain on the same seeds
    spin           -0.008 +- 0.029    27 up, 33 down    p = 0.519
    straightness   +0.032 +- 0.065    not significant
    chatter        -0.001 +- 0.014    not significant

Wind destroys the advantage -- 16 up against 18 down is a coin flip where plain
is 28 against 10 -- and it does not stabilise anything here.

WHICH MEANS THE WIND RESULT WAS NARROWER THAN REPORTED. Its stabilisation was
measured against behav_smell, which carries whole-level odour, and it does not
reproduce against plain. So the effect requires whole-level odour to be present
as well, having already been shown to require the retina intact. A result that
needs two other conditions to appear is a three-way interaction, not the
general course-stabilisation mechanism the paper claimed. The abstract and the
wind section are corrected accordingly.

THE ANSWER. The plain corrected configuration is the model to ship:

    --wide --eye-map anatomical --fixed-turn --phasic-mdn
    --spiking-t4 --optic-gain 16 --yaw-source DNp15 --touch --smell

Every addition tested over the last three days costs something measurable:

    whole-level odour     +7.53 -> +3.80; a direction-free scalar, so more of
                          it is drive without information
    dendritic cables      keeps the score, removes the dependence on vision --
                          a mirrored retina then scores the same as an intact
                          one
    a neck                lifts the frozen arm by turning the eyes across a
                          held frame, so the no-vision control stops being one
    wind                  destroys the advantage on plain; its benefit exists
                          only alongside whole-level odour
    all of them together  p = 0.743 against chance, i.e. nothing

That is the finding, and it is worth more than any single component: the most
anatomically faithful configuration is not the best-working one. Each addition
is defensible as biology and each costs performance, interpretability, or both.
The plain configuration is not a compromise or an ablation -- it is what
survives measurement.
