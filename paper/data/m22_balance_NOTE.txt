A BUMP IS A RATIO, AND THE RATIO CAN BE SET

M21 swept a scalar on one pathway over 256 fold, twice, and could not produce a
heading signal: driving the visual gateway silenced the compass because ER is
EPG's inhibition, and driving the recurrent loop saturated it at the refractory
ceiling. The conclusion was that a bump is not an AMOUNT of activity but a
RELATIONSHIP, and that no single number can create a relationship.

That was a claim with an obvious test. Scale excitation onto EPG and PEN by
g_e, scale inhibition onto EPG by g_i, and sweep them INDEPENDENTLY so the
ratio moves rather than the total. The prediction was fixed before the run: a
bump should appear over a RANGE of ratios and fail on both sides, which is a
signature no magnitude sweep can produce.

  g_e   g_i   ratio   EPG Hz   PEN Hz   ER Hz    bump
   16   0.5    1:0.03  384.24   399.76    8.97   0.000   saturated
    4   0.1    1:0.03  342.01   384.65    8.92   0.020   saturated
    4   8      1:2     237.19   371.57    7.91   0.165   saturated
    2   8      1:4      29.25   183.23    1.87   0.784   LOCALISED
    4  32      1:8      20.64   304.25    2.10   0.856   LOCALISED
    2  32      1:16      0.99   154.81    0.57     --    silent
    4 128      1:32      2.22   296.80    1.55     --    silent
    1   8      1:8       0.17     3.48    0.00     --    silent

THE SIGNATURE APPEARED. Between ratios of about 1:4 and 1:8, with enough
absolute excitation to matter, 78 to 86 percent of the ring falls quiet while
the population runs at 20 to 29 Hz. Above that ratio it goes silent, below it
saturates. That is a window, it fails on both sides, and it is the first time
anything in this model's central complex has held the right KIND of state
rather than switching between dead and flat out.

Note that the ratio alone is not sufficient: g_e 1 with g_i 8 is the same 1:8
and is silent. Both the balance and the absolute level have to be right, which
is what makes this two numbers rather than one.

AND IT IS STILL NOT A COMPASS. In both localised regimes the heading
correlation is nothing -- -0.175 at 1:4 and -0.038 at 1:8. The bump does not
encode where the animal is facing.

The reason is in the same table. ER sits at 1.87 and 2.10 Hz in those regimes:
the visual anchor is still silent. A ring attractor with no input holds A bump,
which drifts; it takes landmark input to hold a bump that means something about
the world. And M21 already showed that ER cannot be driven into life without
killing EPG, because the synapses that would wake it are the same ones that
inhibit the compass.

So the account is complete, and it is a two-part failure rather than one:

  the ATTRACTOR can be repaired by setting a ratio -- two numbers, not one
  the ANCHOR cannot, because ER's drive and its inhibition of EPG are the same
      connection scaled together, and no scalar separates them

TWO LIMITS I CANNOT ARGUE AWAY.

Localisation is not contiguity. This measures that few cells are active, not
that the active cells sit NEXT TO EACH OTHER around the ring, which is what
makes a bump a bump. Testing that needs each EPG's wedge position, which the
annotation does not carry. What is shown is a localised pattern, not a proven
spatial bump.

And the bump metric needed a floor. Without one, a population with a single
cell firing at 0.2 Hz has 50 of 51 cells "quiet" and scores 0.98 -- the same
degeneracy that made M19's population-vector sharpness read 1.000 for a silent
ring. Any sharpness measure is meaningless when there is nothing to be sharp.
The rows marked silent above are exactly the rows that scored 0.98 before the
floor was added, and they are not bumps.
